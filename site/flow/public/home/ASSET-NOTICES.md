# Homepage assets

These assets accompany the FLOW homepage. Source URLs record provenance and
the adaptations used in this copy.

## Fonts

Font files live in `site/theme/fonts/`. Each is distributed with its SIL Open
Font License 1.1 notice, published under `/font-licenses/` during assembly.

| Face | Source | Notice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenRunde Regular and Semibold | [OpenRunde](https://github.com/lauridskern/open-runde) | [open-runde-LICENSE.txt](/font-licenses/open-runde-LICENSE.txt), retaining The Inter Project Authors' copyright |
| JetBrains Mono | Repository's self-hosted Fontsource 5.3.0 copy | [jetbrains-mono-LICENSE.txt](/font-licenses/jetbrains-mono-LICENSE.txt) |
| Quicksand Bold | Repository's self-hosted wordmark font | [quicksand-LICENSE.txt](/font-licenses/quicksand-LICENSE.txt) |

The Jig logo embeds its Quicksand face, covered by the same Quicksand notice.

Paths below are relative to `/home/`.
